## Exact contract

- Hard dependency: V08.1.
- Purpose: implement accounts, bilateral/multilateral balanced posting,
  currency, and claim/debt interfaces.
- Acceptance: every posting balances; cash/asset/liability cannot change
  unilaterally.
- Owner: World Financial Posting is the sole authoritative owner of financial
  account, position, posting-batch and posting facts under ADR-02.

The minimum model is a versioned `FinancialAccount` with owner, country,
settlement asset/currency and account class; an exact `FinancialPosition` at a
WorldVersion; and an immutable `PostingBatch` containing two or more exact
debit/credit legs, V07 Command/Event causation, counterparty identity and one
WorldVersion increment. Claim/debt accounts expose explicit claim and
counterparty identity without implementing issuance, interest, maturity,
default, banking or monetary policy.

## Arithmetic and decision boundary

V08.2 uses `Money`/`WorldDecimal` and canonical strings only. For each currency
in a posting, exact total debits must equal exact total credits. JS numbers,
epsilon/tolerance, silent clamp and partial application are forbidden.

ADR-08 is not a current gate because this step introduces no rounding, FX,
minor-unit conversion, price/formula arithmetic, tax, interest or periodic
cash-flow calculation. Any such need stops the step at
`READY_PENDING_ADR_08`. ADR-07 construction/GDP semantics are outside scope.

## V07/V09 compatibility

Every batch belongs to one V07 transition through Command ID, non-empty Event
IDs, SimTime and WorldVersion before/after. Exact duplicate intent returns the
existing result; a reused identity with changed canonical intent fails closed.
No second financial history is created. V09 later atomically commits Command,
Transition, Events, Inventory/Financial Postings, Receipt, WorldVersion and
Outbox and owns lease, fencing, writer and recovery.

## P0/P1 risks

P0: unbalanced or partially applied posting; unilateral cash/asset/liability;
cross-currency netting; duplicate effect; tolerance/rounding without ADR-08;
second financial truth; direct cross-domain mutation; V07 incompatibility;
premature V09 mechanics; migration divergence or production mutation.

P1: ambiguous account class/counterparty/claim identity; incomplete causation
or version metadata; unclear signed-position convention; later package behavior
silently embedded in the foundation.

## Scope exclusions

- no V08.3 opening seed/reconciliation;
- no inventory mutation or settlement coupling;
- no loans/deposits creation behavior, reserves, OMO, TGA, taxes, bonds,
  guarantees, interest, maturity, default, FX, CPI, prices or GDP;
- no rounding/minor-unit/formula policy;
- no V09 lease/fence/writer/atomic coordinator/recovery;
- no migration publication, production access, UI or Engine implementation.
