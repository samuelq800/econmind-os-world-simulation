## Contract and boundary

`planning/r2_steps.json` and `prompts/steps/V23.3.md` require read-only
risk/scoring, account-error isolation, mechanism explanation and a versioned
score. Constitution R078–R080 require CPI contributions to reconcile to
headline, derived indicators to be read-only, and scores never to feed back
into productivity, trade, FX or credit. Master E18 requires risk flags from
real gaps/ratios/events and scores only after accounts have settled.

- Recompute V23.2 current-account and fixed-basket CPI from caller-supplied
  facts; do not accept unverified serialized results. A GDP mismatch or
  invalid account/CPI fact produces an `ACCOUNT_ERROR` candidate with no
  score or risk flag. It is not converted into a risk metric or economic
  action. Unknown programming exceptions still propagate.
- Only mirror three concrete account quantities for this preparation:
  production GDP, current account and CPI basket-cost change. Each metric
  fact binds its exact current value to the recomputed account output, plus
  initial value, period/currency/accounting version, source/initial receipt
  and snapshot lineage. No synthetic extra GDP/CPI truth is created.
- Caller-owned, versioned candidate policy facts specify exact threshold
  comparisons for risk flags, and a transparent score base, per-metric
  direction/slope and downward-only guardrail cap. The Core kernel computes
  exact signed baseline deltas and contributions; it chooses no hidden
  weight, threshold or formal score policy. Risk explanations list actual,
  threshold, comparison, triggered state and causal fact/receipt references.
- Score is a derived candidate only, never Command/Event/Posting or a
  writable economic input. Candidate score version is explicit and bound to
  policy evidence; ADR-15 must still approve formal score version and CPI
  causal attribution. CPI contributions remain accounting, not causal.
- Deterministic replay re-executes from the same facts and compares the full
  result, not merely a recomputable public hash.
