# V20.3 — Historical settlement and FX reconciliation foundation plan

**Candidate state:** `FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`
**Hard dependency:** V20.2

## Exact scope

- Lock historical trade cash at its execution LC/GCU rate, rounding decision,
  execution time and snapshot lineage.
- Revalue separately with a new caller-provided current rate and decision. The
  locked cash settlement is never altered or re-denominated by current FX.
- Reject mismatched country/currency/time, forged/stale canonical lineage and
  a record whose locked cash does not recompute from its locked rate.

## Exclusions

No trade execution, posting, accounting close, persistent revaluation,
Command/Event/receipt, recovery, database/migration/API/UI/worker, production
or status/Gate authority.
