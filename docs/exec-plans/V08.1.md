## Ledger and Posting ownership

Subject to ADR-02 approval, V08.1 owns the canonical inventory primitives:

- `InventoryAccount`: World owner, country, commodity and approved
  location/bucket identity;
- `InventoryPosition`: exact Quantity in AVAILABLE, RESERVED or IN_TRANSIT;
- `InventoryPostingBatch`: immutable versioned causation and the complete set
  of inventory legs;
- `InventoryPosting`: immutable signed quantity, unit, account/bucket,
  counterleg, Command/Event causation and batch identity;
- reservation, release and dispatch interfaces that produce a complete posting
  proposal, never direct cross-domain balance mutation.

The inventory posting service is the only planned writer of inventory
positions and postings. Resource/Production/Trade/Office/UI code may submit
typed intent or consume derived views/events but may not maintain or write a
parallel authoritative balance. Financial account/posting ownership is
reserved to V08.2 and opening provenance/reconciliation to V08.3.

## Conservation and state-transition invariants

For each World, commodity, unit, ownership boundary and posting batch:

- the canonical exact sum of signed posting legs is zero;
- AVAILABLE, RESERVED and IN_TRANSIT are mutually exclusive position buckets;
- reservation moves AVAILABLE to RESERVED without changing total stock;
- release moves RESERVED back to AVAILABLE without changing total stock;
- dispatch moves RESERVED to IN_TRANSIT without creating or destroying stock;
- no successful operation leaves a physical position negative;
- insufficient stock returns the canonical failure and produces no partial
  posting, position, Event, receipt or WorldVersion effect;
- exact duplicate Command identity/fingerprint returns the stored V07 result
  and creates no second reservation, posting or Event;
- same identity with changed authoritative intent is
  `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` with zero effect;
- correction is a new immutable posting/Event, never UPDATE/DELETE of history;
- unit, commodity, World, account, batch and version mismatches fail closed;
- location, reservation, title, risk and economic recognition remain separate
  and cannot be inferred from a bucket transition before ADR-05 approval.

Constitution commodity conservation remains:

```text
OpeningInventory + Production + ImportsDelivered
- Consumption - ExportsDelivered - Losses = ClosingInventory
```

V08.1 supplies the shared inventory posting primitives, not the later Engine
rules that generate every term in that identity.

The V08 package also requires the later V08.2 financial layer to preserve
canonical exact Money, balance every committed debit/credit batch within one
settlement asset, and forbid unilateral cash/asset/liability changes. Legal
negative equity must remain distinct from illegal negative physical stock.
V08.1 must keep its inventory batch/causation/version shape compatible with
that future symmetric posting model, but it implements no financial position
or Money transfer.

## Exact arithmetic

- Quantity inputs and successful results stay in the validated Foundation
  120-digit public domain and canonical serialization.
- Addition and subtraction are exact. An exact result outside the domain is
  rejected as `DECIMAL_RESULT_OUT_OF_RANGE`; it is never rounded, clamped or
  converted through JavaScript `number`.
- Conservation uses canonical exact equality, never epsilon, tolerance,
  display precision or floating-point approximation.
- Property tests extend the shared Foundation arbitrary and independent BigInt
  coefficient/scale oracle. They do not create a second arithmetic oracle.
- No price-times-quantity, Money, FX, minor-unit or rounding rule is introduced
  in V08.1.

## P0 and P1 risks

### P0 blockers

- duplicate allocation or any negative physical position;
- unbalanced or partially committed inventory posting batch;
- nondeterministic/tolerance-based conservation or replay;
- duplicate Command causing a second posting/Event/effect;
- mutable committed postings or Events;
- Trade/Engine/UI/projection directly writing authoritative inventory;
- ADR-02/ADR-05 implementation before owner approval;
- migration-chain divergence, false provenance or production mutation;
- importing V09 writer/lease/fencing semantics into V08.

### P1 majors

- ambiguous unit/location/bucket identity that cannot yet create a negative
  balance but permits future semantic drift;
- incomplete causation/version metadata or unclear operational versus
  authoritative fields;
- missing restart/rehearsal coverage on the required non-production surface;
- API/receipt error mapping that obscures the canonical zero-effect result.

Any P0 or P1 finding affecting a downstream contract stops continuation and
requires correction under the central review policy. V08.1 is P0 and cannot
self-award `VERIFIED`.
