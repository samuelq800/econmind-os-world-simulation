## Planned ownership and behavior

- One logical authoritative writer lease exists per World.
- Acquiring or superseding a lease allocates a monotonically increasing fencing
  token; renewal cannot reduce or recycle it.
- Every authoritative commit carries the current holder identity and fencing
  token and rechecks them inside the database transaction.
- An expired holder, superseded holder or stale token is rejected even if the
  process still believes it owns the lease.
- WorldVersion remains an independent optimistic concurrency guard: a valid
  lease does not authorize committing from an old WorldVersion.
- API/browser surfaces cannot acquire the economic writer role. The Worker owns
  lease acquisition, renewal and recovery.
- Lease wall time is operational liveness data only; it cannot change economic
  ordering, SimTime or replay results.

## Expected persistence surface

V09.1 now has the forward `0005_world_v2_writer_lease_fencing` candidate in the
sole V02 migration chain. It owns a per-World lease/fence record, guarded
renewal/takeover transitions, and a transaction-time lease/fence/WorldVersion
guard. It does not insert Event, Posting, receipt, outbox or current
materialization facts; those all-or-zero economic writes remain V09.2.

`0006_world_v2_writer_lease_lineage_guard` is the narrow forward remediation
for Review B finding V09.1-BLK-01. It rejects direct DELETE and TRUNCATE of the
lease record, so a Worker-role mutation cannot erase the fence lineage and
recreate token `1`. Normal expiry/takeover remains the sole route to a new
fencing token.

The candidate remains branch-local and unpromoted until its own schema evidence
and governance gates pass. Its immutable source commit, historical path and
SHA-256 are bound in `database/migrations/manifest.json`.

The migration evidence must identify the migration ID, full source commit,
historical path and exact hash; rehearse clean and existing databases; and cover
RLS/grants. Production publication remains a separately authorized Main Site
operation.

## Test architecture

P0 tests use real PostgreSQL and independent connections/processes:

- two writers race for one World and only the current fenced holder commits;
- an expired lease holder cannot commit;
- a superseded or stale fencing token cannot commit;
- a current lease with stale WorldVersion cannot commit;
- renewal, process restart and lease takeover preserve monotonic fencing;
- deterministic results remain identical across restart and takeover.

P1 tests cover lease-renewal boundaries, transient connection failure,
observability without secret leakage, and multi-World isolation.

Current evidence is recorded in
`docs/reports/V09/V09.1_IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE.md`. Local clean/existing
PGlite rehearsal and disposable CI PostgreSQL evidence pass; ADR-18's dedicated
non-production staging RLS/grant evidence remains `NOT_RUN`. This is therefore
not a step verification or V09.2 entry decision.

The fail-closed staging harness contract is recorded in
`docs/reports/V09/V09.1_STAGING_EVIDENCE_HARNESS.md`. It is dry-run by default,
requires an exact owner-approved dedicated target and secret admin PostgreSQL

`world_v2` namespace and three dedicated non-login roles.

The original candidate's independent review evidence is preserved in
`docs/reports/V09/V09.1_REVIEW_B_CHANGES_REQUIRED.md`; the forward fix requires
