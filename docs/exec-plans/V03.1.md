# Execution plan for V03.1

## Authority and scope

- Sources: Constitution exact-value rules; Master and Office numeric requirements; ADR-08.
- Goal: isolated decimal.js constructor and canonical Money, Quantity, Price, Rate, and ID foundations.
- Exclusions: Engine formulas, currency minor units, settlement-specific rounding, and V06 time engine.

## Gate and risk

- Dependencies: V00.3/V01.3 verified; implementation stays in the Gate A candidate.
- Effective risk: P0, deterministic authoritative numeric semantics.
- ADR-08 remains unapproved; only parsing, finiteness, arithmetic ownership, and serialization are frozen.

## Change and validation

- Accept exact strings, reject non-finite/malformed inputs, and prevent number round-trips.
- Test currency/unit mismatches, Rate bounds, closure, and explicit non-authoritative conversion.
