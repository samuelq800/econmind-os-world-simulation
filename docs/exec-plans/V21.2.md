# V21.2 — Tariff, Customs, and Shipment Foundation Candidate

## Status and boundary

`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`. V21.1 and V10.4 are `PLANNED` at the
candidate baseline, so this is not an order-book, reservation, stock, customs,
ledger, command, event, API, worker, database, RLS, UI, or production
implementation.

## Scope

- Resolve a caller-owned `IMPORT` or `EXPORT` request through concrete active
  sanction/ban, quota/export-cap, treaty, bilateral, and general facts.
- Return a pure eligibility result: blocked requests have zero permitted quantity;
  tariff precedence is treaty, then bilateral, then general only when unblocked.
- Assess a tariff once per explicit assessment reference and expose an inert
  collection eligibility result; no tariff posting is created.
- Bind every source fact and output to the existing immutable provenance/replay
  contract, exact units, snapshot tick and canonical payload.

## Owners and exclusions

Trade remains the future owner of controls, customs and shipment lifecycle;
Finance/ledger remains the future tariff-receivable/posting owner; Inventory is
the future stock owner; V21.1 is the future order/contract owner. There are no
default policy rates, quota reservation, payment, order creation, or inventory
effect in this candidate.

## Evidence target

Focused tests cover tariff precedence, quota headroom, ban/sanction blocking,
one-assessment rejection and evidence replay equality. Required local checks:
Core typecheck/build, focused regressions, boundary and secret checks.
