## Contract

V12.3 is the R2 step “资源 Reconciliation 与 Stress”. It provides pure
per-resource conservation assertion, deterministic sequence replay, stale-link
rejection, exhaustive-depletion checks, and inventory balance reconciliation.
The foundation must prove that initial geological endowment never increases and
that every accepted movement contains its resource identity, exact unit and
decimal, causal references, and before/after balances.

`reconcileCommodityInventory` is a typed identity helper for a caller-supplied
opening balance and explicit physical flows. It does not decide which flows are
authorized, their simulation time, their ordering, or their durable commit.

## Concurrency and recovery boundary

The pure functions reject a transition or inventory movement whose predecessor
reference is stale, which provides an explicit compare-against-lineage boundary
for later integration tests. It is not single-writer enforcement, a lease,
fencing, a transaction, durable idempotency, or crash recovery. Those remain
V09.3-owned and are still blocking evidence for product integration.

## Required focused test matrix

- five-layer and geological conservation, including exploration-only movement;
- exact decimal rendering and resource/commodity unit mismatch rejection;
- extraction-to-inventory causal-link and ownership-boundary checks;
- reserve and contract-reservation constraints, losses, and reconciliation;
- deterministic replay, duplicate reference and forged/stale-link rejection;
- repeated exact extraction through exhaustion without negative pools.

This is a local pure-core evidence set. It neither changes
`status/progress.json` nor claims `VERIFIED`, a Gate decision, V12 package
