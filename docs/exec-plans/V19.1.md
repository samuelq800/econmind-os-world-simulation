## Purpose and exact ownership

- Implement a deterministic commercial-bank ledger calculation for paired loan
  origination and repayment, deposits, NPL classification, and balance-sheet
  identity.
- Banking balance facts remain caller-owned, externally attested snapshots.
  This module creates no account, command, Event, receipt, posting, durable
  ledger, World State, or second source of truth.
- Every input fact must bind a stable source reference, version, snapshot,
  snapshot hash, predecessor fact(s), and simulation-time snapshot. Every
  result carries exact money transitions and a canonical replay preimage.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- All amounts use canonical exact decimal `ExactMoney` with one canonical
  currency per calculation. No float, unit-erasure, implicit conversion,
  rounding, rate, interest, default, or policy choice is introduced.
- Loan origination increases loan assets and deposit liabilities by the same
  caller-supplied principal. Repayment is explicitly either deposit
  cancellation or a settlement-asset transfer; both preserve the bank
  balance-sheet identity.
- NPL is a bounded classification of existing loan assets, not a hidden
  macro adjustment or replacement ledger.
- ADR-02 is respected: future Banking owns bank balances and future Central
  Bank owns reserve-account facts. ADR-08 and ADR-09 remain unresolved; the
  foundation therefore consumes explicit caller facts and does not calculate
  rates, rounding, policy thresholds, approval requirements, or defaults.
