# V14.1 — E11 Technology Rights and R&D Foundation-Mode Candidate

## Status and boundary

`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`. This is a pure Core rights and research
calculation, not the start of V14.1.

## Scope

- Bind caller-owned `MASTERED`, `LICENSED`, `TRANSFER`, `JOINT`, or `RESTRICTED`
  right facts and research inputs to immutable provenance and replay evidence.
- Preserve `LICENSED` as distinct from `MASTERED`; only an already mastered
  caller fact may carry explicit mastery evidence.
- Calculate R&D output and progress without changing the category, granting a
  technology right, creating capacity, or supplying a policy formula.

## Acceptance target and dependencies

An unexpired, constrained licence can be evaluated for permitted build use but
cannot fake mastery. Completed R&D cannot directly add capacity. V14.1 retains
V05.3, V06.3, V08.3, V11.3, V12.3, and V13.3 as normal-mode dependencies.
