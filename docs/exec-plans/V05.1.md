# Execution plan for V05.1

## Authority and scope

- Sources: V01 identity whitelist, Constitution server authorization, and six Office specs.
- Goal: validated identity facts, current membership/country/Office assignments, and server-side capability checks.
- Exclusions: live main-site integration, browser role authority, RLS publication, and economic Commands.

## Gate and risk

- Dependencies: V02.3/V03.3 are in the same unmerged Gate A candidate.
- Effective risk: P0, identity and authorization.
- ADR-02/19/20 remain unapproved; adapters are interfaces with synthetic tests only.

## Change and validation

- Accept only `user_id`, `display_name`, and `school_id` identity facts.
- Reject unauthenticated, suspended, removed, unassigned, cross-country, cross-Office, legacy-role, and forged-client claims.
