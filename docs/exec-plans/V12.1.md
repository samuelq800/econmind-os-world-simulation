# V12.1 — Geological resource pools and exploration foundation candidate

**Candidate state:** `FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`
**Immutable baseline:** `5356fe93932eb285b3c21977a655e4c6e7bb6746`
**Execution boundary:** pure `packages/core` only

## Contract and dependency cut line

V12.1 is the R2 step “地质资源五层与 Exploration” in work package V12,
E08 Resource & Inventory. Its recorded hard dependencies are V08.3, V09.3,
and V11.3. V09.3 is not closed and ADR-04 remains
`PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED`; this candidate is therefore a reusable pure-core
foundation only, not V12 product implementation, a V12 state advance, or a
Gate-ready result.

The candidate implements the exact E08 layer order:

```text
fixed GeologicalEndowment
  = UNDISCOVERED + DISCOVERED + RECOVERABLE + DEVELOPED + EXTRACTED
```

Exploration is the sole operation that may move `UNDISCOVERED` to
`DISCOVERED`. It moves an exact caller-supplied quantity between those pools;
it cannot create endowment, infer discovery output, choose a time phase, or
select a recovery/extraction formula.

## Authority and ownership

- E08 is the sole proposed owner of geological pools, deposits, cumulative
  extraction provenance, commodity inventory, strategic physical reserves, and
  in-transit commodity ownership. This follows Constitution R040–R044 and
  Master U0737–U0762.
- E10 receives only a typed, read-only E08 availability boundary. It cannot
  extract a resource, change an E08 pool, or consume a reserve through this
  candidate.
- Every transition carries a validated transition reference, predecessor
  reference, source reference, exact decimal quantity, required resource unit,
  and frozen before/after pool snapshots. These are inert pure results, not
  Commands, Events, durable receipts, or authoritative writes.

## Scope

The implementation is confined to:

- `packages/core/src/resource-inventory/` and its public core export;
- one focused world-core test suite;
- V12 plans, traceability, implementation reports, and test evidence.

No database/RLS/migration/API/worker/UI/Command/Event/production path is
created. No seed, country, deposit calibration, rate, price, cost, wage,
macro, conversion, or rounding formula is selected. ADR-04 prevents a
time/read-order decision; ADR-08 prevents formula-specific quantity treatment;
ADR-17/V09.3 continue to own durable commit, idempotency, and recovery.

## Acceptance evidence

The pure candidate must reject non-forward pool moves, unit mismatch,
non-positive or source-exceeding movement, duplicate transition references,
and forged predecessor links. Deterministic replay of the same initial state
and transition sequence must reproduce the same state and transition records.

Independent review remains required before any `VERIFIED`, integration,
promotion, or product claim.
