## Preparation scope

1. **National strategy:** fixed primary/supporting strategy catalogs;
   maintain/change/suspend actions; explicit political-capital cost; no direct
   GDP, productivity, trade, public-support or credibility buff.
2. **National priorities:** fixed primary/secondary catalog, required distinct
   priorities and fixed change reasons; explicit traceable political cost.
3. **Cabinet agenda:** structured issue fields, one lead office, distinct
   supporting offices, constraint/target references and a maximum of three
   simultaneously active issues.
4. **Cabinet proposals:** exact proposal version, owner and required approval
   references, read-only fiscal/FX/political/administrative costs and five
   Captain decisions. Approval produces a governance decision candidate only;
   it never applies owner policy settings.
5. **Political capital:** seven fixed allocation buckets. Reallocation is
   exactly zero-sum. Action costs debit one bucket and available capital while
   increasing spent capital by the same amount; Captain cannot create capital.
6. **Government commitments:** fixed statement/commitment levels, structured
   target/deadline rules and explicit political cost. Credibility and public
   support effects are absent and reserved for later system calculation.
7. **Cabinet coordination:** fixed conflict types and coordination actions,
   explicit office/agenda/proposal references and political cost. Outputs are
   coordination candidates, never Finance/CB/Industry/Trade/Social mutations.

## Traceability and invariants

- Bind every input to source/predecessor references, source version, snapshot
  hash, canonical payload and integer simulation tick.
- Preserve exact `political_capital` before/delta/after transitions; reject
  insufficient buckets, negative/non-canonical values and non-conservation.
- Bind proposal decisions to an exact integer `proposal_version`; no approval
  survives a material version change by implication.
- Recompute module-specific results from bound facts during replay and compare
  the entire proof; never trust a caller-recomputed public hash alone.
- Reject any request containing direct fiscal, monetary, production, inventory,
  trade, GDP, productivity, credibility or public-support mutation fields.
