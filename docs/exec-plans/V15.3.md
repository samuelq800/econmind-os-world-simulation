## Scope

The E04/E05 foundation inputs keep teacher/staff people, service cases,
medicine doses, beds and simulation time separately typed. Every result carries
input IDs, output IDs and an exact before/after replay trace. Person counts are
whole and may not be inferred from money.

## Invariants

- Education admits/graduates only within teacher, seat and budget capacities.
- Healthcare delivery cannot exceed staff, facility, supplies, budget or demand.
- The public-service kernels never create a duplicate staff allocation or a
  direct wellbeing/productivity effect.

## Evidence target

trace identity uniqueness. No database, authorization, event or settlement test
is in scope.
