## Exact contract

Freeze one versioned scheduler priority registry and the total order
`(dueSimTime, priorityRank, scheduledEventId)`. No caller or Engine may supply
an ad-hoc numeric rank. Network arrival, database row order, object iteration,
wall-clock races and player-online state are never tie-breakers.

Freeze the Constitution-mandated versioned 15-stage orchestration registry in
the exact order from `CONSTITUTION-U0190` through `CONSTITUTION-U0204`:
Execute Scheduled Events, Update Stocks, Resource Production, Energy
Production/Allocation, Industrial Production, Labour Market, Household Income
& Consumption, Public Services, Domestic Market / Prices, Global Market &
Trade, FX & Financial Markets, Fiscal Settlement, National Accounts, Risk &
Crisis Detection, and Score & Notifications. Preserve E01-E18 as separate
Engine IDs and provide a versioned explicit Engine-operation-to-Stage mapping
contract. Do not register or claim future E02-E18 economic operations.

Freeze the command-cutoff boundary as the immutable World SimTime observed
when the worker begins an authoritative transaction. This step records the
boundary value only; V07 owns command/event schemas and V09 owns durable
transaction execution.

## Ownership and exclusions

The affected authoritative owner remains the single E01 clock/scheduler in
`packages/core`. Inputs are V06.1 branded SimTime, V06.2 scheduled-event state,
approved registry IDs and an explicit transaction-begin action. Outputs are
immutable ordered work, stage metadata/mappings and a cutoff snapshot. The
step adds no economic writes, E02-E18 reducers, command ledger, database/RLS,
migration, production scheduler, UI authority, randomness or production
mutation.

## Validation and exit

Tests cover permutation invariance, equal-time priority and lexical ties,
rejection of ad-hoc/unknown priority or stage IDs, preservation of all E01-E18
identities, exact Constitution stage order, immutable cutoff capture, day/year
edges, paused due work, RUNNING catch-up and canonical power-loss
serialize/restore. The same canonical inputs must produce the same work order
without player-online input. Required checks are focused V06 tests/properties,
V06.1/V06.2 regressions, architecture/boundary scanners, governance validation,
environment safety and the applicable full repository baseline. V06.3 ends
`IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`, then execution stops before V07 for
`V06_PACKAGE_REVIEW`.
