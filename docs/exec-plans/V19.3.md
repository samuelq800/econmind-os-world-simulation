## Dependency and purpose

V19.3 normally depends on V19.2. Its isolated foundation scope adds no product
status claim; it only tests and exposes the deterministic failure boundaries
of the V19 pure-Core candidate.

The candidate distinguishes a liquidity shortfall from a capital/solvency
shortfall using explicit caller-supplied required reserve and required capital
facts. ELA may address a positive reserve shortfall only when the bank has no
capital shortfall. It never changes equity and can never erase negative equity.

## Required failure paths

- forged, altered, stale, mixed-lineage, duplicate, or replayed facts;
- cross-bank or cross-currency fact mixing;
- insufficient deposits, securities, collateral, facility capacity, or
  reserves;
- invalid NPL bounds and an unbalanced Commercial Bank or Central Bank ledger;
- negative equity/capital shortfall on an ELA request; and
- any tool invocation without an explicit, valid input fact and trace binding.

## Explicit exclusions

No resolution, recapitalisation, interest accrual, haircut calculation,
default/rounding formula, approval workflow, policy decision, scheduling,
durable idempotency, or authoritative write is implemented. Those remain owned
by their later approved packages and their authoritative writers.

## Exit condition

Run the V19 focused failure/invariant vectors together with Core build,
typecheck, lint, boundary/pattern, safe-environment, secret, formatting, and
diff checks. Freeze and push only a candidate that remains
`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`, then stop for independent review.
