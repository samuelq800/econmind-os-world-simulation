## Pure calculation boundary

- Reuse, do not duplicate or edit, V23.1's production-GDP and expenditure
  comparison. Require exact C/I/G/X/M with production mismatch error, never
  average or overwrite either value.
- Add caller-fact eligibility evidence per expenditure category, matching
  amount/receipt and settlement class. C is settled final consumption, I real
  capital formation from construction value actually created, G settled
  public-service consumption, X/M delivered ownership transfer. Reject
  transfer, intermediate purchase, project approval/commission, contract or
  shipment classification as final GDP expenditure. Source authenticity is
  future writer responsibility, not asserted by Core.
- Current account = X − M + net primary income + net secondary transfers.
  Primary/secondary inflow and outflow facts bind country/period/currency,
  source receipt and closure predecessor set. Transfers affect current
  account, not production or expenditure GDP. Missing source sets are not
  silently zero; empty sets require explicit period-complete closure fact.
- CPI uses exactly Food, Energy, Housing, General Goods and Services, one
  fixed positive quantity per category and versioned base/previous/current
  observed price facts. Compute exact basket costs and category cost deltas.
  Report base-relative index and period inflation/contribution ratios as
  exact numerator/denominator pairs, avoiding unapproved decimal rounding.
  Contribution numerators must sum exactly to headline change numerator,
  all with the same previous-cost denominator. No causal inflation claim is
  inferred from accounting contributions.
- All facts bind source, predecessors, same snapshot hash/version/tick,
  explicit country/period/currency/accounting or basket version and receipts.
  Provide deterministic replay preimages and recompute from facts on replay.
