## Pure candidate contract

- Internal cabinet brief facts carry explicit country, season, world,
  classification, version, scope and source receipt. Caller-attested viewer
  facts carry authenticated actor, cabinet/office membership and
  server-authorization reference. Only an explicitly matching current
  brief-version grant can reveal restricted content. Unconfirmed/expired
  access yields a generic denial object with no brief ref, title, body,
  source bindings or hash preimage; the server must also gate its fetch
  before calling Core. No UI-only hiding is treated as security.
- Policy facts declare target, direction, exclusive resource, exact
  simulation-time interval, version, owner, command and receipt. Detect
  contradictory overlapping target directions and competing exclusive
  resource claims deterministically; return only diagnostics, never silently
  override policy or mutate an economic field.
- Decision facts produce deterministic, metadata-only responsibility-log
  candidates with actor/office, version, decision, tick, outcome and source
  receipt. No classified brief content enters the log and no append occurs.
- A temporary crisis power fact and crisis-lifecycle fact determine active
  permission over `[issuedAt, min(expiresAt, crisisEndedAt))` using exact
  non-negative integer simulation-millisecond ticks. At the boundary it is
  expired; a finished crisis cannot leave the power active. This logic does
  not grant real access or consume ambient `Date.now()`.
- All admitted facts bind source, predecessors, snapshot hash/version/tick
  and canonical payload. Replays recalculate full results from the same
  facts; public hash preimages alone are not proof of authorization.
