## Change plan

- **Authoritative owners affected:** none. `apps/world-web` is the sole
  changed runtime and remains a renderer; it receives no authority.
- **Reads and writes:** reads an imported static fictional atlas only; writes
  no browser storage, network request, cache, database or server state.
- **Commands, events, receipts and postings:** none.
- **Database, RLS, environment and time:** none; no environment variables,
  credentials, timer, real-time subscription or external network call.
- **Legacy-system impact:** none; no code or asset is copied from the original
  EconMind site. The map is newly generated fictional geometry.
- **Map contract:** use a clearly labelled local planar CRS
  (`FICTIONAL_ATLAS_KM_V1`) with a 36,000 km × 18,000 km canvas. Every route
  owns its polyline; its displayed distance is the deterministic sum of that
  same polyline's segments. Land routes are sampled against fictional land
  polygons; sea routes are sampled against fictional water after their port
  endpoints. The geography uses several separated, medium-sized landmasses,
  island arcs and polar fragments rather than a two-supercontinent layout. A
  central barrier landmass leaves named narrow passages as the only prepared
  through-corridors for the relevant sea routes. Every continental plate has
  one larger south-eastern satellite island, while archipelagos are small and
  deliberately few. Inland lakes are small local features, not inland seas.
  Mountains, straits, basins, small river deltas, plains, hills, grasslands
  and rivers carry display-only resource tags with no reserve, quantity or
  economic effect. Mountain ranges must be linked to a named collision belt;
  latitude bands, subsurface warm-current paths and seismic belts are likewise
  static explanatory layers, not a climate or disaster engine. Ports, rail
  corridors, mountain passes, inter-island bridges/tunnels, pipelines and
  large logistics/energy sites are a distinct display-only infrastructure
  layer; they do not mean an asset exists in the World State or has capacity.
- **Resource plausibility guard:** each resource label must declare its physical
  origin and pass a map-only compatibility check: collision orogeny for mapped
  copper/iron, sedimentary basins for oil/gas, evaporitic basin context for
  lithium, and non-cold alluvial plain/basin/delta context for grain. This is
  a visual-geology constraint only; it neither estimates nor allocates any
  tonnage, yield, reserve, price, population or port capacity.
- **Visual-fidelity rule:** the renderer may use deterministic, local SVG
  terrain/ocean texture, relief and colour layers that share the atlas
  coordinate system. It must not use an unregistered raster image as a
  measurement surface, because a visually attractive but geometrically
  different image would make route lengths false. The generated concept image
  is documentation-only art direction. Cities, factories, mines, farms,
  refineries, ports and energy/logistics sites are position/type annotations
  only and cannot assert construction, ownership or capacity.
- **2026-09-16 terrain redline:** the first high-fidelity satellite-terrain
  concept is explicitly **not accepted as an atlas layout**. Its four-corner
  symmetry, evenly provisioned landmasses, unstructured climate placement,
  inconsistent snow lines, arbitrary deserts and river braiding cannot inform
  a future World. The replacement visual concept must use an intentionally
  asymmetric macro-geography: one large resource-rich but coast-poor
  continental mass, one long continental margin, one inland-facing landmass,
  one concentrated archipelago/trading region and a small number of distant
  strategic islands. It must show continuous latitude-led climate progression;
  snow lines controlled by latitude and relief; deserts caused by subtropical
  pressure, rain shadow, cold current or continentality; dendritic source to
  delta river basins; a true endorheic inland sea with inflows and salt-basin
  margins; a single explainable plate-boundary system of trench, volcanic arc,
  collision range and earthquake belt; varied shelf/coast types; and three to
  five visually legible global maritime choke points. Static weather is
  excluded. The 70 future visual territories must be placed only after that
  physical layout is accepted and must express resource complementarity rather
  than a self-sufficient inventory per region. Any imagery remains a
  non-metric visual layer; route centre-line geometry remains the sole basis
  for measured distance.
- **Physical derivation contract:** the map is produced in this fixed causal
  order, rather than by adding attractive independent layers:
  `tectonic plates → mountain ranges / plateaus → drainage basins and river
systems → latitude → prevailing winds + ocean currents → precipitation →
climate → vegetation → agricultural suitability → geological mineral
potential → settlement attractiveness → cities → ports → measured trade
corridors`. A downstream map object must name its upstream physical basis.
  This contract is still visual planning, not a World State, country seed or
  economic allocation. Population, capacity, reserves and country strength
  remain absent while the physical map is being rebuilt.
- **V2 preservation / V3 physical-reconstruction brief:** retain the existing
  left supercontinent, central arid interior, eastern maritime margin and
  southern-island macro silhouettes. Do **not** replace the world with a new
  AI-generated layout. The revision must instead: break the northern wall of
  snow into a principal chain, high plateau, passes, foreland basin and river
  valleys; place the western subtropical desert behind an explicit coastal cold
  current / pressure belt and grade it through semi-arid grassland; replace
  the western many-equal-channel delta with tributaries that converge to one
  main river then split only at the delta; feed the central endorheic salt lake
  from mountain sources via oasis river corridors; move asymmetric relief and
  coastline structure off the central landmass's concentric centre; vary
  snowline by both latitude and elevation along the eastern margin; designate
  its western cyan water as a broad continental-shelf sea (fishery, reef and
  offshore-energy potential), while giving another coast a narrow/deep shelf;
  reduce islands into a few named volcanic-arc, hotspot, continental-fragment
  and coral groups; retain one exceptional equatorial high volcano but remove
  snowcaps from ordinary tropical islands; add an internal, non-rendered
  latitude grid and cold-to-warm-to-cold climatic buffer before the southern
  polar ice; define basin-scale ocean gyres; and reserve at least six named
  global choke-point geometries. Mountain and river rendering must use a
  consistent 70% physical / 30% game-emphasis scale. Economic geography must
  prevent western self-sufficiency, give the central interior energy/mineral
  comparative advantage, and make the eastern margin resource-importing,
  port/manufacturing/fishery oriented. These are causal display constraints,
  not production economic outcomes.
- **Horizontal-wrap / vertical-composition correction:** this is a global
  equirectangular-style fiction map, so the left and right borders are the same
  meridian seam, not two visual endpoints. The terrain composition must be
  driven primarily north-to-south: northern high-latitude land and relief,
  mid-latitude continental interiors and dry belts, an equatorial humid
  archipelago/choke-point band, southern temperate ocean and an Antarctic
  buffer. The two horizontal borders must visually continue one another through
  ocean-current direction, shelf/deep-ocean treatment and, where present,
  a seam-crossing landmass or island chain. No opposing left-versus-right
  continental-poster composition may be used.
- **Rejected axial variant:** a first horizontal-wrap edit that placed most
  continental land in a northern band and most islands in a southern band is
  also rejected. Geographic wraparound must not collapse into a top-land /
  bottom-islands poster. The accepted direction is an oblique, interleaved
  tectonic arrangement: unequal continental margins, a central dry interior,
  a diagonal maritime margin, an equatorial-but-not-straight island arc,
  southern temperate continental fragments and only limited remote islands,
  all crossing more than one latitude zone.
- **Current terrain-candidate record (not a V25 acceptance):** the latest
  visual-only candidate uses a vertical reflection of the preceding asymmetric
  plate layout. Its polar fringe is therefore at the northern edge; latitude
  effects are still derived continuously from the vertical axis, just with the
  north/south presentation inverted. It now has four unequal land poles: a
  southern continental core, a central shelf-and-arc complex, a rifted
  maritime fragment, and a lower-left humid strait archipelago. The eastern
  continental cape extends further south without becoming a long island. The
  left and right edges remain one open-ocean meridian. This raster is a
  selection candidate only: it does not certify any country geometry,
  infrastructure location, route, climate datum or resource allocation.
- **Raster/vector alignment guard:** legacy illustrative feature, route and
  infrastructure coordinates must never be described as a calibrated V7
  overlay merely because they can be rendered over the new terrain image.
  After the owner accepts a terrain layout, re-author the local vector coast,
  drainage, city, infrastructure and corridor fixture from that same local
  geometry; measure all new corridors only from those replacement polylines.
  Until then the image is a visual candidate and the existing local vectors
  remain reusable test fixtures, not V7 geographic evidence.
- **Terrain refinement / layer contract:** the latest clean terrain candidate
  reduces visibly snow-covered peaks to the exceptional high ranges and polar
  fringe; replaces branch-heavy white peak webs with continuous ridges and
  lower brown-grey relief; makes the principal rivers, a closed basin and one
  compact delta legible; softens desert margins through a semi-arid steppe;
  and removes current-like technical dashes from the raster. It deliberately
  contains no resource, city, industry, port, route or political symbols.
  Physical annotations, climate, ocean currents, trade routes, resources,
  political display boundaries and infrastructure are independent local UI
  layers and default to off, so the physical base remains readable.
- **Seventy-boundary display contract:** the 70 visual territories are
  presentation-only boundary sketches, not countries in the V27 sense. They
  may be `INLAND`, `COASTAL` or `ISLAND_GROUP`; every `ISLAND_GROUP` contains
  a single or paired display-island count and a dashed visual sea envelope
  containing its capital marker. That envelope is not a legal claim, EEZ,
  sovereignty classification, national ID or database record. The validator
  rejects missing or malformed island envelopes and any such display field on
  a non-island territory.
- **Coastline-boundary display guard:** non-island display territories name
  one of four local V8 land-boundary regions (southern core, rifted fragment,
  northern core or central shelf). The Political layer clips their drawn
  boundaries to the corresponding hand-traced fictional coast mask, so an
  outer boundary cannot visibly continue across open water. Island groups are
  intentionally excluded from those masks and instead use the one-or-two-island
  visual envelope. These masks are disposable presentation geometry, not a
  replacement land dataset, official country border, territorial-water claim or
  authorisation to start V27.
- **Geographic opportunity guard:** no fictional polity receives an economic
  score, resource quantity or capacity. The static fixture instead limits
  geographic concentration: every polity must have a measured network link;
  mapped large grainland is inland and does not receive a prepared sea port;
  and the map test suite checks the plate/island, strait and small-lake
  constraints. Cities, agricultural areas, mineral areas and roads will have
  exact local coordinates and measured geometry. Population, crop output,
  mineral reserves and production values are explicitly
  `UNASSIGNED_BY_V27`: economic initialisation and balancing remain V27 work.
- **Expected files:**
  `apps/world-web/src/map-lab/*`, `apps/world-web/src/App.tsx`, focused map
  tests under `tests/world-web/`, a non-runtime concept image under
  `docs/assets/V25.1/`, and V25.1 implementation/evidence reports.

## Validation plan

- **Positive tests:** exact polyline-distance calculation; stable formatting;
  both land and sea corridors; fictional features and resource tags render;
  fallback UI contains no live-data dependency.
- **Negative tests:** reject unknown endpoints, non-finite coordinates,
  mismatched declared distance, a land route sampling water, a sea route
  sampling land, and a resource tag that tries to carry an economic quantity.
- **Invariants and security checks:** every ID is unique; all geometry is
  inside the local atlas extent; only the fixture is read; no `fetch`, storage,
  persistence, credentials, command or mutation import is introduced; the
  original site remains untouched.
- **Commands to run:** pinned-toolchain focused Vitest, world-web typecheck and
  build, targeted ESLint/Prettier, repository boundary and authoritative
  pattern checks, `git diff --check`.
- **Evidence vocabulary:** `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT_RUN`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Exit condition

The candidate may demonstrate a loss-tolerant fictional map shell, but it does
not validate MapLibre/deck.gl/PMTiles/MVT or authorised projections. Those
items remain `NOT_RUN` until a dependency and classification decision permits a
separate bounded experiment. No V25 status/progress entry will be edited.
