## Calculation contract

- Reuse V22.1's fixed activity names and immutable active-contract facts. Each
  calculation must bind its supplied term fact to the contract's structured
  `valueFactRef`, exact current signed version, contract state and parties.
- Require explicit source, predecessor, snapshot/version/hash and integer
  simulation-millisecond evidence for contract, terms and balances.
- Commodity agreement: paired quantity transfer and GCU cash transfer.
  No current price or FX selection; caller supplies exact quantity and cash.
- FDI: investor-to-project GCU cash plus issued-share/foreign-ownership
  transitions; total issued shares must equal domestic plus foreign shares.
- Sovereign loan: paired GCU cash, lender asset and borrower liability with
  equal bilateral debt before and after.
- Infrastructure finance: caller chooses debt or equity mode. Both have paired
  cash; debt has equal claim/liability, equity has issued-share/ownership
  transitions. Core chooses no funding mode or ratio.
- Resource development: paired cash plus transfer of a bounded, exact
  development-right share. Geological endowment is unchanged.
- Return before/delta/after traces with causal input references and canonical
  replay preimages. Reject duplicate execution witnesses and insufficient
  balances, cross-country/currency/unit mismatch, stale lineage or terms, and
  one-sided economic effects.
