# V13.2 — E10 Production Foundation-Mode Candidate

## Status and boundary

`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`. This candidate is a deterministic pure
Core calculation, not V13.2 product implementation.

## Scope

- Bind installed operational capacity, operating duration, productivity,
  V12 read-only material availability, E09 delivered energy, labour, and
  logistics to one immutable replay snapshot.
- Calculate one physical output at the weakest explicit material, energy,
  labour, or logistics bottleneck.
- Emit proposed exact material, energy, labour, and logistics consumption
  transitions before the single output transition; no stock or output is
  posted by this kernel.

## Acceptance target

Energy or material scarcity must cap output and consumption must exactly scale
with that output. No input is silently created and no actual output is emitted
twice.

## Dependencies retained

V13.2 retains V13.1 as its normal-mode hard dependency. No sector policy,
price, allocation priority, or productivity default is introduced.
