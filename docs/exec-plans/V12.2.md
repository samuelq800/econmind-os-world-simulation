**Dependency:** V12.1 foundation candidate only; no product dependency is declared closed

## Contract

V12.2 is the R2 step “开发/开采/储备/损耗”. It extends the same inert E08
foundation with exact `RECOVERABLE → DEVELOPED → EXTRACTED` transfers and
commodity-inventory bucket boundaries. Geological `EXTRACTED` is cumulative
depletion provenance; its equal extraction output is recorded in E08 commodity
inventory without treating the two differently scoped facts as a second source
of truth.

Each extraction requires an exact developed-pool transition and an inventory
movement whose source reference is that transition. A forged source reference,
resource/commodity mismatch, unit mismatch, stale predecessor reference, or
duplicate movement reference rejects before any result is returned.

## Inventory and ownership boundary

E08 inventory uses explicit, same-unit physical buckets:

- usable;
- strategic;
- reserved for contract;
- inbound and outbound in transit; and
- cumulative losses as auditable depletion provenance.

Only typed E08 functions can move usable stock to strategic reserve or contract
reservation. Strategic stock can only return to usable stock through an
explicit release transfer; this foundation exposes no free reserve-consumption
operation. E10 receives a read-only usable-availability view and cannot perform
E08 extraction, reserve, or inventory mutation.

## Exclusions and deferred authority

There is no extraction-capacity, recovery-rate, price, cost, labour, energy,
equipment, infrastructure, loss-rate, production, trade, or strategic-target
formula. There are no durable transfers, imports, exports, consumption,
Commands, Events, approvals, transactions, or reservations across writers.
Those require their named owners, ADR-04 time/read-order decision where
applicable, and V09.3 atomic/recovery implementation and evidence.

The candidate records exact physical arithmetic only and remains
`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`.
