## Dependency and scope boundary

V19.2 normally depends on V19.1. Within this one owner-authorized isolated
candidate it consumes only V19.1's pure fact/trace surface; this does not
advance the repository's NORMAL-mode V19.1 status or authorize V19.2 product
work. No status, Gate, mainline, production, database, migration, API, worker,
command, Event, receipt, or UI path is in scope.

## Purpose

- Calculate exact, provenance-bound Central Bank and commercial-bank traces for
  OMO purchase/sale, reserve and capital compliance inputs, collateral-bound
  regular refinancing, ELA, and central-bank balance-sheet postings.
- Derive monetary base, M1, and M2 only from supplied Central Bank and Banking
  ledger facts. No aggregate is stored or accepted as a second source of truth.

## Guardrails

- Each tool receives all economic terms as explicit caller facts: transaction
  amount, collateral eligible value, facility capacity, required reserve,
  required capital, and any policy/term reference. It does not select policy
  rates, interest, haircuts, reserve/capital ratios, maturity, defaults, or
  rounding rules.
- OMO and facility traces preserve both balance sheets and cross-owner reserve
  reconciliation. Sales reject insufficient securities or reserves.
- Refinancing and ELA reject mismatched bank/currency/snapshot facts,
  insufficient collateral or capacity, and malformed/replayed evidence.
- ADR-02 owner separation is retained. ADR-08/ADR-09 are not implicitly
  resolved: this module validates caller-supplied facts but has no approval
  resolver or policy formula.

## Evidence plan

Focused vectors cover balanced OMO, reserve reconciliation, M1/M2 derivation,
facility capacity/collateral failures, cross-bank/currency mismatch, replay
integrity, and no mutation of a caller-owned ledger. Evidence is recorded only
as an immutable non-production candidate, with all persistence and product
validation marked `NOT_RUN`.
