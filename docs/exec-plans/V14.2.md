# V14.2 — E12 Project Lifecycle Foundation-Mode Candidate

## Status and boundary

`FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`. This is a deterministic project-input
calculation, not a project command, approval, payment, construction record, or
V14.2 product implementation.

## Scope

- Bind project, approval, funding, V12 read-only material, labour, technology,
  oversight, and commissioning facts to one immutable snapshot.
- Calculate bounded construction progress from explicit caller-owned factors.
- Emit exact material and labour consumption proposals with before/delta/after
  continuity; no inventory, worker, project, payment, or facility state changes.

## Acceptance target and dependency

Approval or funding alone cannot create progress or capacity. Construction is
bounded by explicit inputs and V14.2 retains V14.1 as its normal-mode hard
dependency.
